I'm **N01SX**, a high-school student interested in the intersection of **software development, algorithms, and Linux systems**.

I enjoy understanding how things work under the hood just as much as building things people can use. My approach is simple: stay curious, solve real problems, and keep improving the details.

- **Currently learning:** C++, data structures, algorithms, and software design
